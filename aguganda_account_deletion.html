Request Account and Data Deletion

Afterschool Geekery Uganda
Developed and operated by Peter Mekis

You can request deletion of your Afterschool Geekery Uganda account and the personal data associated with it by sending an email to the app developer, Peter Mekis.

How to submit a request

  1. Email mekis.peter@gmail.com from an address where you can receive a reply.
  2. Include your name and the phone number used for your account so that the relevant account can be identified.
  3. We may contact you to verify your identity before completing the request.

Open a pre-filled deletion request email

Sample email

Subject: Afterschool Geekery Uganda account and data deletion request Hello, I request deletion of my Afterschool Geekery Uganda account and associated personal data. Name: [your name] Phone number used for the account: [your phone number] Thank you.

What will be deleted

After the request is verified, the account and personal data associated with it will be deleted or anonymized. Some information may be retained where necessary for safeguarding, security, reporting integrity, or legal obligations. If any information must be retained, the reason will be explained in the response to your request.

Privacy information

For more information about how data is processed and retained, see the Afterschool Geekery Uganda Privacy Policy.
