# Afterschool Geekery Uganda — Closed Test Guide

Thank you for testing the Afterschool Geekery Uganda app. This test follows one
complete workflow from administration through mentor reporting and back to
administrative review. It should take approximately 20–30 minutes.

Please use fictional test data only. Do not enter real children’s names,
personal information, or private photographs.

## Before you begin

1. Install the app using the Google Play closed-test link.
2. Remain opted into the test and keep the app installed for at least 14 days.
3. Ask the test coordinator for your tester number, from `01` to `15`.
4. Prepare four non-sensitive test images:
   - three different images for a session;
   - one image for a story.
5. Record anything confusing, broken, slow, or visually awkward.

Use your tester number in every name you create. For example, tester `03`
should create `Test Mentor 03`, `Test Course 03`, and `Test Student 03`. This
prevents testers from confusing one another’s records.

## Test accounts

### Administrator

Ask the test coordinator for the administrator test account's phone number and
password. Do not use a production account.

### Shared mentor account

Ask the test coordinator for the shared mentor test account's phone number and
PIN.

The shared mentor account is available for general exploration. The main test
path below uses the new mentor account you create yourself.

## Main test path

### A. Set up a mentor and course as administrator

1. Open the app, choose **Administrator**, and sign in with the administrator
   test account.
2. Open **Manage mentors** and choose **Add mentor**.
3. Create a mentor using:
   - First name: `Test`
   - Last name: `Mentor NN`
   - Phone: `07000000NN` (for example, tester `03` uses `0700000003`)
   - Country ID: `1`
   - Preferred language: English
   - Temporary PIN: `123456`
   - Active: on
4. Return to the admin menu, open **Manage courses**, and choose **Add course**.
5. Create a course using:
   - Name: `Test Course NN`
   - Description: `Closed testing course created by tester NN`
   - Country ID: `1`
   - Day: today’s weekday
   - Starting time: any convenient time
   - Active: on
6. In **Manage courses**, select the new course, choose **Assign mentors**, tick
   `Test Mentor NN`, and save.
7. Log out.

Replace `NN` with your two-digit tester number throughout.

### B. Report activity as the new mentor

8. Choose **Mentor** and sign in with the new mentor’s phone number and
   temporary PIN `123456`.
9. When prompted, replace the temporary PIN with another six-digit PIN. Keep
   it until testing is complete.
10. Open **My profile** and confirm that the mentor name and phone number are
    correct.
11. Open **Manage courses** and confirm that `Test Course NN` is visible.
12. Open **Manage students**, choose **Add student**, and create:
    - First name: `Test`
    - Last name: `Student NN`
    - Country ID: `1`
    - Birth year: any reasonable fictional year
    - Gender: any option
    - Courses: select `Test Course NN`
13. Return to the mentor menu and open **Log a session**.
14. Select `Test Course NN` and today’s date.
15. Select `Test Mentor NN` as a teaching mentor.
16. Enter a project, select the test student as present, complete the remaining
    outcome fields with short test answers, and submit the session log.
17. Open **View session logs**, select the new log, and choose **View**.
18. Open `Test Student NN` from the log and confirm that the student record is
    displayed.
19. Return to the log, choose **View or upload photos**, select exactly three
    non-sensitive test images, and choose **Upload three photos**.
20. Return to the mentor menu and open **Stories**.
21. Choose **Submit story**, select `Test Course NN`, enter a short fictional
    success story, add the fourth test image, and submit it.
22. Open **Curriculum**, expand a category, and open one chapter.
    - On Android, confirm that the chapter loads in the app.
    - On the web, confirm that the chapter opens in a new browser tab.
23. From the mentor menu’s top bar, open **Privacy & support** and confirm
    that the privacy-policy link works.
24. Log out.

### C. Review the submissions as administrator

25. Choose **Administrator** and sign in again with the administrator test
    account.
26. Open **View session logs**, find the new log, open it, and confirm:
    - the course, date, mentor, project, and attendance are correct;
    - `Test Student NN` opens from the log;
    - all three session photographs are visible.
27. Open **Stories**, find the new story, and confirm its text and photograph.
28. Choose **Deactivate**, confirm the action, and verify that the story is
    marked **Inactive**.
29. Log out.

## Optional exploration

If you have time, also try:

- editing the mentor, course, or student you created;
- filtering mentors, courses, students, logs, photos, or stories;
- opening **Track students** and reviewing the test student;
- viewing photos through **View photos**;
- submitting and reviewing a **Course visit** report;
- rating another mentor’s story;
- viewing the story-of-the-month archive;
- testing the app after closing and reopening it.

Do not deactivate records created by other testers.

## Feedback

Please send the coordinator:

1. Your tester number and phone model.
2. Whether you completed sections A, B, and C.
3. Any step that failed or was unclear.
4. One thing you liked.
5. One thing you would improve.
6. Screenshots of errors, if any.

Even a short response is useful. For example:

> Tester 03, Samsung A14. Completed A, B, and C. Everything worked. Uploading
> photos was clear, but finding the session log took me a moment.
