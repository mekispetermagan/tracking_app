Privacy Policy

Afterschool Geekery Uganda
Effective date: 30 July 2026

Afterschool Geekery Uganda is a restricted project-management app for authorized adult mentors and administrators. It is not intended for use by children and it contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

1. Who is responsible

The app is independently developed and operated by Mekis Péter in support of the Afterschool Geekery Uganda educational program. Privacy, support, and deletion requests may be sent to mekis.peter@gmail.com.

2. Information processed

Depending on a user's role and the features used, the app may process:

  • mentor and administrator profile information, including names, phone numbers, country, role, active status, and course assignments;
  • student program records, including names, country of origin, birth year, gender, active status, and course assignments;
  • course and session information, including locations, schedules, attendance, mentor roles, projects, achievements, operational issues, and session notes;
  • student progress records, including completed projects, skill activities, and learning observations;
  • course-visit reports, including attendance, learning and enjoyment observations, safety concerns, follow-up actions, and support provided;
  • stories, ratings, monthly selections, and photos submitted by authorized users;
  • authentication and security information, including password or PIN hashes on the server, access tokens, and account status; and
  • the last-used login phone number saved on the user's device for convenience.

The app does not request device location, contacts, microphone access, advertising identifiers, or broad access to the photo library. When a user submits a photo, the system photo picker provides access only to the selected image.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to operate and improve the educational program, including managing courses and participants, recording attendance and activities, tracking learning progress, supporting mentors, reviewing safeguarding concerns, selecting program stories, producing internal reports, maintaining account security, and resolving technical problems.

4. Who can access information

Access is restricted by account role. Mentors receive access needed for their assigned program work. Administrators receive broader access needed to manage and supervise the program. Data may also be processed by infrastructure providers that host the application and its backups. Information is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with data brokers.

5. Children and photos

The app is operated by authorized adults, but some records concern children participating in the program. Users must enter and upload only information and photos they are authorized to process for program purposes. Children's records and images must not be used for unrelated personal, commercial, or advertising purposes.

6. Storage and security

Production traffic is transmitted over HTTPS. Authentication credentials are not stored as readable passwords or PINs on the server. Access tokens are stored using the device's secure storage. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

Records are retained while needed for program operation, safeguarding, monitoring, reporting, continuity, or legitimate legal and administrative purposes. Accounts and courses may first be deactivated to preserve the integrity of historical reports. Information that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymized, subject to legitimate retention needs.

8. Access, correction, and deletion requests

Mentors can review and update parts of their profile in the app. Requests to access, correct, deactivate, or delete an account or associated personal data can be made by emailing mekis.peter@gmail.com. Please identify the relevant account and request. Identity may need to be verified before a request is completed. Some records may be retained where necessary for safeguarding, reporting integrity, security, or legal obligations; any such limitation will be explained.

9. International processing

Users and program participants may be located in different countries from the app's developer or hosting infrastructure. Information may therefore be processed across national borders with safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, program practices, or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this address with its effective date.
